Ring Smash!

Drag curved pieces onto a round board. Fill a ring or a slice — and smash it! Chain combos, spin a ring to save the day and unlock 21 delicious themes.

Coming soon to iPhone, iPad and Android.

Easy to learn, hard to put down

A fresh twist on block puzzles: everything happens on a circle.

Fill & smash

Complete a full ring, or a slice from the center outwards, and watch it burst into pieces.

Spin a ring

Stuck? Turn a ring with your finger to line up the gap. Spin smashes score double.

Combo chains

Smash again and again for Nice!, Great!, Amazing! — up to Legendary combos.

21 themes

Charge the star for a super blast and a surprise theme: watermelon, sushi, lava, clockwork…

About us

Vubbo Labs is a small independent game studio. We mix bright colors, satisfying feedback and simple rules into games that are easy to start and hard to put down — like a little science experiment that always ends with a happy “pop!”.

Ring Smash! is our first game. We build it with care for every player: fair difficulty, short sessions that fit any break, and themes made to make you smile. Thank you for playing!
