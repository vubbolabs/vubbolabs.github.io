Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how Vubbo Labs ("we") handles information in the mobile game Ring Smash! (the "Game").

Summary

The Game does not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob; AdMob may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Best score, number of games played and recent scores.
  • Settings such as sound, vibration, language and unlocked themes.
  • Gameplay statistics used to tune difficulty and ad frequency (for example how often a "continue" offer is accepted), together with a broad country group derived from your device's region setting and your device's memory size. These values stay on your device and are not sent to our servers.

Uninstalling the Game deletes this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions in order to deliver, personalize, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game.
  • On iOS, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this in your device's Settings.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

We may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played (for example when a game ends or a screen is opened). This information is used in aggregate to improve the Game and is not used to identify you.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we do not keep personal information on our servers, most requests can be handled on your device (for example by changing ad settings or uninstalling the Game). For questions about data processed by Google, please see Google's privacy policy. For anything else, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
